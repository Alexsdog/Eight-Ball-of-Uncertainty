FAQs

1. What is the Eight Ball of Uncertainty?

The Eight ball of Uncertainty is a mystical decision-making toy engineered to give only vague, indecisive responses such as “Maybe,” “Possibly,” or “Hard to say.” It is perfect for people who enjoy suspense, ambiguity, or avoiding responsibility.

2. Does the Eight Ball of Uncertainy ever give clear answers?

Absolutely… not. Or maybe. Hard to tell. In general, it is specifically designed to never provide definitive yes or no responses. If you need clarity, you may want to consult a coin flip instead.

3. What kinds of questions should I ask it?

Any yes/no question is acceptable—just don’t expect closure. Ask about love, life, luck, lunch plans, or philosophical dilemmas. It will reply with uncertainty regardless.

4. Is the Eight Ball of Uncertainty accurate?

It’s impossible to verify. The Eight Ball of Uncertainty specializes in plausible deniability. If its answer “Maybe” turns out wrong, it also could have been right… potentially… sort of.

5. Can I rely on it for important decisions?

Perhaps. (Translation: No. Please don’t.)

6. Does it ever break or jam?

If it stops working, just assume it’s still thinking. Or hesitating. Or being emotionally unavailable. A gentle shake usually convinces it to resume its ambiguous duties.

7. Is the Eight Ball of Uncertainty suitable for children?

Potentially. It contains no harmful components, but children may become philosophers much earlier than expected.

8. Can I use it to predict the future?

Possibly in theory, but realistically… probably not. But then again… who knows?

9. What if I need a decisive answer right now?

The Eight Ball of Uncertainty encourages patience, reflection, and accepting that life is confusing. But if you truly need certainty, try a different device—like a certified adult.
